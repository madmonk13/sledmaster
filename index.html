How to play

Launch: drag back on the screen, aim, release.
Steer: ←/→ or A/D, or drag left/right.
Boost: hold Space or Shift, or touch the screen with a second finger (needs a rocket).
Tricks: in the air, tap ↑ for a front flip, ↓ for a back flip, ← to spin left or → to spin right (hold ←/→ to steer), or flick the screen. Finish before you land or you crash. Chain tricks in one jump for 1x, 2x, 3x… and they pay out as coins.
Ice is slippery and fast. Wings let you glide off hills.
Snowmen: smash through them at speed, but hit one slowly and you stop dead.
Gas cans: rare, and only once you own a rocket. Each one refills half a tank.
Birds: flocks cross the course up high. Hitting one costs speed and lift for a moment.
Penguins: fast enough and you bowl them over; too slow and they hold their ground.
Trees, igloos & polar bears: solid. Hit square-on and you stop; clip the edge and you glance off.
Sound: M or the speaker button to mute.
